For Prospective Guests

Considering coming on The Making Of Hosted By Jack Pitts?

The show is for founders, operators, and family-business owners who have spent years building something. I want to hear how you started, the calls you got wrong, and what changed as the business grew.

We record remotely for about an hour, and there's almost nothing to prepare. We'll cover where you started, the hard years, and the decisions that got you here. Episodes go up on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and YouTube, and I share every one on LinkedIn.

One session. All you have to do is show up and talk.

How it works
  1. i.A quick hello. Send a short request. If it's a fit, I'll reply and we'll set up a time.
  2. ii.One session. About an hour, recorded remotely, lightly edited.
  3. iii.It goes live. Up on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and YouTube, and I share it on LinkedIn.
Before you reach out

Who is a good fit

Founders, operators, and family-business owners who have been at it for years and are happy to talk openly about it. If you've had to make payroll and figure things out as you went, you'll be in good company.

Who is probably not a fit

If you mainly want to announce a launch or pitch a product, this isn't the right show. I'm more interested in how you got here than in what you're selling this quarter.

What we usually talk about

How you got started, the first few years, the mistakes, the money, and the moments the business changed direction. We usually finish with what you'd tell someone just starting out.

How to prepare

Almost nothing. You don't need slides or a script. Think of two or three stories you remember well and don't mind sharing. We can swap notes on the angle beforehand if you want, but most guests skip it.

What happens after recording

I lightly edit the episode and publish it to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and YouTube. You get the links and a share card for your own channels, and I post it on LinkedIn too.

About the Host

"I work in finance, private equity, so I deal with business owners all the time. I was like, hey, why not do a podcast where I get to tell their stories?"

Jack Pitts, in the Riverside-Brookfield Landmark

Jack Pitts is the founder of Salt Creek Advisory, an AI-native deal sourcing and investment banking practice. Chicago-raised and Vanderbilt-educated, Jack is based in the Chicago area and spends his days around founders, operators, and investors. He started The Making Of Hosted By Jack Pitts to capture the stories he's always wanted to hear.

The show takes its inspiration from NPR's How I Built This, with more focus on the operators and family-business owners whose companies you pass every day without knowing the story. You usually hear about the overnight success, not the year a company almost failed. Jack goes looking for that year.
