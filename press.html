The show

The Making Of Hosted By Jack Pitts is an entrepreneurship podcast. Each episode is a long-form interview with a founder or business owner about how they built their company. Episodes are published on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, and YouTube.

About the host

Jack Pitts co-founded Salt Creek Advisory, a sell-side M&A investment bank, with his brother, Connor, and leads client relationships and buyer outreach. He previously worked in private equity business development at Blue Wolf Capital and Kingfish Capital and in investment banking at ButcherJoseph & Co. He graduated cum laude from Vanderbilt University and is the founder and CEO of HelmIQ.
